We are the technic turtles, a First Lego League robotics team. Thank you for visiting our website! we have been a team for about three years, and we are very exited to show you what we have done! Scroll down to see our history.

Our Seasons

Submerged 2024-25 season

In the 2024-25 season, we were challenged to help marine biologists. We researched and designed an underwater base to allow them to stay underwater for extended periods of time so they can research up close.

Unearthed 2025-26 season

In the 2025-26 season, we were challenged to solve a problem for archeologists. We designed and built the Super Siever to improve the sieving process at dig sites.
The build instructions are available, here.

Bioglow 2026-27 season

In the 2026-27 season, we were challanged to help biologists protect biodiversity. We came up with three projects to help.
These can be veiwed, here (Seed Collector), here (Game), and, here (Advocacy Work).
