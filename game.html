Native Plant Game

The native plant game was made with one purpose: to teach kids about the importance of native plants and biodiversity, and to make sure that they have fun at the same time. To achieve this goal, we created Turtle's Garden, a kid-friendly, relaxing garden game that will inspire anyone who plays it to protect our native plants!
