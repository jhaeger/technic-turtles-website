Seed Collector

Collecting seeds can be challenging. We designed the Seed Collector to make seed collecting easier. You will need in a 3D printer. If you don't have one, you could check your local public library to see if they have one you can use. The Seed Collector prints with few supports, and the heads attach to a tweezer-type body with dovetails. The seed collector has successfully worked on Mexican Hat, Sideoats Gramma, and the beefsteak plant. Collecting seeds is important to help preserve native blackland prairie plants. Blackland prairie plants have good benefits, and we should all do our part to protect them.
